Why do the tools need an account?

The app never needs an account and never will. The tools are bigger and newer, and an account lets us see who uses them, so we can keep improving them and keep them free. Your designs are not stored by us: they are saved where you choose.

Once you have signed in on a device, the tools keep opening there for 30 days, even without a connection.
