LT-Planner

Coming later. A garden and farm planner in 2D and 3D: shape the ground, lay out beds, paths, fences and seed-raising areas, plan what grows where and when, and see it through the season. Plans go to the app as simple beds linked to your calendar.

Like LT-Print, it will be free with a Lineage Tracker account, and plans will be saved to your own Drive, Dropbox or device.
