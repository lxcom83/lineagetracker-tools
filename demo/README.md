# Plant generator (lab spike, 2026-10-17) - NOT wired into the planner, NOT for upload yet
- plants.js: spec (JSON, version 1) -> one merged geometry with vertex colours. Seeded, growth stage 0.1-1, 7 presets (apple, oak, willow, pumpkin vine, black pumpkin, tomato, corn, sunflower) built from 4 engines: tree, vine, bush, stalk. Fruit is its own shape spec (width, height, ribs, shoulder taper, dimple, stripes, unripe/ripe/secondary colour).
- lab.html: overhead locked orthographic view, growth slider, sun direction/height with real shadows, shadow toggle, 1/50/200/1000 plant stress test (instanced).
- vendor/: Three.js 0.186.1 unchanged (MIT). LICENSE (MIT) covers our code in this folder only.
- Measured (headless software rendering, no GPU, so fps is meaningless): 8 plants = 42k triangles; one mature tree about 8-14k triangles; 200 plants instanced 604k; geometry build 10-60 ms. Real tablet fps still to be measured on the Samsung.
- v2 (polish pass): shaped leaves per species (ovate, oval, lobed oak, willow lance, heart, strap, palmate pumpkin, compound tomato) with midrib, side veins, curl and droop; fruit with ribs, dimples, stems, calyx, blush, corn husks, sunflower head. Lab has Show (one plant or fruit close-up) and Side/Isometric views. Still rough: apple blush has a hard edge, corn cob hidden by husks, tree leaves low detail, no colour UI, no save/load, no LOD.
- 2026-10-20 block 1: quality levels (`generate(spec, stage, {level})`), citrus / blackFutsu / southAnna / pattypan presets, `proxyGeometry`, `specToJSON/specFromJSON`; `field.js` (pure draw-only-what-you-see engine: rules for blocks, five detail levels, caps per quality, ground tiles), `fieldview.js` (three.js instancing), `farm.js` (generated 20 acre farm), lab: Quality, 20 acre farm options. Test: qa-tools/tools/plantfield_test.mjs.
- v3 (21 Oct 2026): engines tree, vine, bush, stalk, rosette and leafy. `leafy` forms: loose (lettuce, spinach), head (cabbage), curd (cauliflower, broccoli), tall (kale), stalks (chard, beetroot, radish), strap (onion, garlic, leek fan), fern (carrot), mound (herbs), low (strawberry). Presets also cover capsicum, chilli, eggplant, potato, bean and pea. Far-away leafy plants use a jagged mound or a tuft of blades. The planner picks a plant per crop with `presetFor(crop, variety)` (model.js); red, rainbow and Tuscan varieties get their own look.
- v4 (22 Oct 2026, look stage 4): 40 fruit, nut, berry and vine plants, one recipe per plant in the plant database (kit/plantdb.js, field `look`). Fruit is drawn where it really grows (`spec.bear`: tips, spurs, along, stalks, chains; see bear()) and in its real shape, size and colour (new shape options: neck for pears, figs and avocados, tip for lemons, flat for mangoes, scales and a leafy crown for pineapples, the flower end's dry parts with `calyxEnd: 'flower'`). New kinds: `palm` (pawpaw: fruit packed round the trunk under the leaves), `banana` (clump, hanging bunch with hands and the purple bell), `climber` on its own posts and wires (`train`: cordon for grapes with hanging bunches, curtain for passionfruit, canopy for kiwifruit), leafy form `pine` (pineapple). Crown shapes (CROWN: round, dome, spread, vase, upright, shrub, canes) and multi-stem shrubs (`stems`). `fit: true` stretches the branch frame so the grown plant is the recipe's height and spread while leaves, fruit and trunk keep real sizes; trees draw leaves from their own random stream so branches match at every detail level. Small fruit use fewer triangles. Checked by plantdb_test.mjs (fruit place matches the database, drawn size within the stated range, builds at every level, under 70k triangles at Low).
